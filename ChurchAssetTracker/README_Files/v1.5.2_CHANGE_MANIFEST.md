## Validation checklist
1. Save Organization settings with optional Website, Email and Phone fields blank.
2. Save only Organization Phone and verify the update succeeds.
3. Upload PNG/JPG/JPEG/WEBP organization logo and verify it persists.
4. Sign out and verify the login logo has no portal-generated white border/background.
5. Sign in and verify the sidebar logo has no portal-generated white card.
6. Verify branding remains readable in light and dark mode.
7. Change Primary/Secondary/Accent colors and verify login + authenticated shell update.
8. Save SMTP settings with optional fields blank and send a test email.
9. Verify existing Documentation/Worship paths remain intact.

## Note on source images
The portal no longer adds a logo background. If the uploaded image itself contains a solid rectangular background, that background will remain part of the image. Transparent PNG/WEBP artwork is recommended when the logo should visually blend into the portal theme.
